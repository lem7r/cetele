Kohort

Support

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Kohort is a goal and habit tracker built around small groups. You join a Kohort, a mentor sets shared goals, and everyone keeps their own running tally. If something is not working, or you just want to ask a question, this page is the place to start.

Get in touch. Email ebvesc@gmail.com and you will normally get a reply within two business days. Kohort is built and supported by one person, so please be patient over weekends.

Common questions

I forgot my password

Kohort does not collect email addresses, so there is no password reset email. Instead, you were given a recovery code when you created your account. Enter that code on the sign in screen to regain access. If you no longer have your recovery code, your account cannot be recovered, and you will need to create a new one.

How do I join a Kohort?

Ask the person who created it for the invite code, then open Settings and choose Join a Kohort. Anyone in the group can find the code in the Kohort settings.

How do I export my data?

Open the Insights tab and scroll to the bottom. You can download your full history as an Excel file or as a CSV. Mentors can export their Kohort's shared goals and member progress from the bottom of the Mentor tab. Members' personal goals are never included in a mentor export.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account, goals, logs, and history. Shared goals you created in a Kohort pass to a remaining member. This cannot be undone, and there is no way to recover a deleted account.

Something looks wrong, or the app will not load

First, close the app fully and reopen it. If the problem continues, email ebvesc@gmail.com with your device model, your operating system version, and a short description of what you were doing. A screenshot helps a great deal.

Reporting a person or a Kohort

If someone in a Kohort is behaving badly, you can leave the Kohort at any time from the Kohort screen, and a mentor can remove a member from the group settings. To report something that needs attention beyond that, email ebvesc@gmail.com with the Kohort name and what happened. Reports are read by a person, not a bot.

Privacy

Kohort collects no email addresses, runs no analytics, and includes no third party tracking. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
