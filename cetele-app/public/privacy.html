Kohort

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Kohort is a goal and habit tracker built around small groups. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, who can see it, and how to get it back or delete it. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

The short version. We do not ask for your email address. We do not use analytics or third-party trackers. We do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone. You can export everything you have, and you can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

Only what the app needs to work:

  • Account details — a username, a display name, an optional short bio, and your choice of one of our built-in avatar images. We do not collect your email address or phone number.
  • Your password — stored only as a cryptographic hash (scrypt). We never store, and cannot recover, your actual password.
  • Your goals and progress — the goals you create and the days you log against them. This is the substance of the app.
  • Kohort membership — which groups you belong to and your role in each.
  • Your time zone — so that "today" means your today, and streaks are counted against the right day.
  • Messages you write — encouragement notes you leave for other members.

What we do not collect

  • No email address or phone number.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party SDKs of any kind.

Who can see your data

Kohort is built around sharing on purpose, so this matters. Every goal you create has a visibility setting that you control, and we honour it:

  • Private goals are visible only to you.
  • Shared goals are visible to the people you choose — your mentors, your Kohort, specific people, or everyone.
  • Kohort goals are shared by design: members of that Kohort can see each other's progress against them. That is the point of the group.

Mentors can export a spreadsheet for a Kohort they lead. That export contains only the Kohort's shared goals and member progress against them. It never includes members' personal goals, regardless of any other setting.

Where your data is stored

Your data is held in a database on servers operated by Render, our hosting provider, and is transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Render can access the underlying infrastructure as our hosting provider; no one else has access.

Getting your data out

You can export everything you have at any time from the Insights tab, as an Excel spreadsheet or a CSV file. It includes every goal and every day you have logged. No request or waiting period is involved.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from within the app, in Settings. Deleting removes your account and the data associated with it. Some content you contributed to a shared Kohort may remain visible to that group in aggregate form where it cannot reasonably be separated from other members' data.

Children

Kohort is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted in the app.
